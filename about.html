Professional profile

Dr. T. T. Subramanian is a poultry veterinary science expert, author and educator with approximately sixty years of association with the poultry sector. His work seeks to convert accumulated scientific and field knowledge into structured, accessible resources for practitioners, students, teachers and industry colleagues.

Purpose of the Toolbox

The Poultry Health Toolbox — India began as an effort to organise poultry-health products into clear practitioner directories. It developed into a decision-support reference that encourages readers to identify the flock problem before selecting an intervention.
